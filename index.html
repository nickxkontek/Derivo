Saving & PDF

Save makes a .derivo file you can reopen and keep editing. Your work is also auto-saved, so it comes back if you close the window.

Export PDF opens the print window: set Destination to Save as PDF. Under “More settings”, untick Headers and footers for a clean page. Equations are saved as sharp vector graphics.

Pasting: text with $…$ or $$…$$ (like notes from an AI chat) turns into real math automatically. Copying math out gives LaTeX.
